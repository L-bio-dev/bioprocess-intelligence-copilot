# Licensing and collaboration

BIC is offered under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 in the root
`LICENSE` file. The project is source-available, rather than open source under
the Open Source Definition. The copyright notice is in `NOTICE`.

## Permitted uses and commercial enquiries

The license permits noncommercial purposes and expressly permits the personal
and organizational uses described in its text. It includes permissions for
educational institutions, public research organizations and other listed
organizations. The license text governs; this summary does not narrow those
permissions or any rights already granted under another applicable license.

Commercial uses outside those permissions require a separate written agreement
with the maintainer, L-bio-dev, unless already authorized by an applicable
license such as the historical MIT grant described below. Commercial use can
include internal business deployment, not only selling copies or a service.

To propose a collaboration or request commercial licensing, open a GitHub Issue
in this repository titled `Collaboration enquiry` or `Commercial licensing enquiry`.
Describe the intended use briefly. Issues are public: do not include confidential
information, credentials or personal contact details. Private discussion can be
arranged afterwards. An enquiry is not permission; terms must be agreed in writing.

Collaboration is welcome. The license does not itself establish a partnership,
compensation arrangement or a requirement to involve the maintainer in permitted
noncommercial projects. Those arrangements require a separate agreement.

## Transition from MIT

The project version at commit
`cca8970cb19c91f22d662520e35ca39f98777bae` was published under the MIT License.
Its license text is preserved verbatim in `LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt`.

The commit introducing this document changes the license offered for the project.
It does not revoke MIT permissions previously granted on released material.
Previously MIT-licensed code remains usable, modifiable and redistributable under
those terms, including for commercial purposes. This also applies to that material
when it remains present in later versions; changing the license file alone does
not create exclusive commercial control over it.

New material contributed under PolyForm is subject to its applicable terms.
The archived MIT text is a record of the earlier grant, not a blanket MIT license
for future additions. Git history is retained to identify the transition.

## Third-party material and future contributions

Third-party dependencies and other separately licensed material retain their own
licenses. This change covers only rights the maintainer is entitled to license.

Before accepting contributions intended for separate commercial licensing,
the maintainer and contributors should explicitly agree on the necessary rights.
Submitting an issue or pull request does not itself assign copyright or grant
unlimited commercial relicensing rights to the maintainer.
